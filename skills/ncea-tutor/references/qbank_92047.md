## 92047-2024-Q1b · 2024 Exam Q1(b) · Q1 total 8 marks · Achieved

- Location: 92047-exm-2024.pdf, Question One (b).
- Topic: gravitational potential energy lost by a falling ball (show that).
- Answer: 8.6 J. ΔEp = mgΔh = 0.0585 × 10 × 14.7 = 8.5995 ≈ 8.6 J. Formula must be shown.
- Verified: independently recomputed (0.0585 × 10 × 14.7 = 8.5995) + matches 2024 schedule ("0.0585×10 ×14.7 = 8.6 J"). HIGH.

## 92047-2024-Q1c · 2024 Exam Q1(c) · Q1 total 8 marks · Achieved/Merit

- Location: 92047-exm-2024.pdf, Question One (c).
- Topic: speed of impact from energy conservation.
- Answer: 17.1 m/s (accept 17 m/s). Ek = ½mv² → v² = 2 × 8.6 / 0.0585 = 294.0 → v = 17.15 m/s.
- Verified: independently recomputed (v² = 294.017…, v = 17.147) + matches 2024 schedule ("v = 17.1 m s–1, accept 17 m s–1"). HIGH.

## 92047-2024-Q1e · 2024 Exam Q1(e) · Q1 total 8 marks · Merit/Excellence

- Location: 92047-exm-2024.pdf, Question One (e).
- Topic: power output of a person running up stairs; definition + assumption.
- Answer: 210 W (accept 211 W / 210.5 W). Ep = mgh = 48 × 10 × 15 = 7200 J; P = E/t = 7200 / 34.2 = 210.5 W. E requires: definition of power (rate of energy transfer) + assumption that all energy goes to gravitational potential (no frictional loss) + unit.
- Verified: independently recomputed (7200 / 34.2 = 210.526) + matches 2024 schedule ("7200 / 34.2 = 210 W, accept 211 W rounded"). HIGH.

## 92047-2024-Q2e · 2024 Exam Q2(e) · Q2 total 8 marks · Merit/Excellence

- Location: 92047-exm-2024.pdf, Question Two (e).
- Topic: average rate of thermal energy absorption (show that it is ≈1.2 W).
- Answer: 1.2 W (accept 1.225 W). ΔT = 7 °C; E = mcΔT = 0.75 × 4200 × 7 = 22 050 J; P = E/t = 22050 / (5 × 3600) = 1.225 W. Excellence requires both the energy value and the power value with units.
- Verified: independently recomputed (22050 / 18000 = 1.225) + matches 2024 schedule ("P = 1.2 W, accept 1.225 W"). HIGH.

## 92047-2025-Q1d · 2025 Exam Q1(d) · Q1 total 8 marks · Merit/Excellence

- Location: 92047-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (d).
- Topic: speed of a falling paint pot from energy conservation + energy loss.
- Answer: 20 m/s. Ep = mgh = 40 × 10 × 20 = 8000 J; 8000 = ½mv² → v² = 400 → v = 20 m/s. Full credit also needs the stated assumption (all Ep → Ek) and the explanation that some energy is lost to thermal energy as air particles hit the pot, so the real speed is less.
- Verified: independently recomputed (v = √400 = 20) + matches 2025 schedule ("v = 20 m s–1"). HIGH.

## 92047-2025-Q1b · 2025 Exam Q1(b) · Q1 total 8 marks · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 92047-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (b), 250 W motor lifting a 40 kg paint pot
  20 m in 35 s.
- Topic: energy supplied by the motor vs gravitational potential energy gained.
  gravitational potential energy of the paint pot when it is at the top of the building" —
  consider the electrical energy provided, consider the GPE gained to 20 m, and explain why
  the two numbers differ.
- Answer: E = Pt = 250 × 35 = 8750 J supplied; Ep = mgh = 40 × 10 × 20 = 8000 J gained

  motor.
- Tier/marks: Achievement — selected the correct equation for either quantity with numbers
  substituted; Merit — mentions energy loss (and both calculations with correct equation and
  working); Excellence — both calculations correct WITH units, AND explains that the loss is
  heat or sound.
- Verified: question verified in 92047-exm-2025.pdf Q1(b); both worked values (8750 J,
  8000 J / 7840 J) and all three tier bullets read directly from 92047-ass-2025.pdf Q ONE (b),
  page 1; arithmetic re-derived. HIGH.
