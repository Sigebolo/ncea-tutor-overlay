## 91390-2025-Q3c · 2025 Exam Q3(c) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91390-exm-2025.pdf, Question Three (c), CaCl2 dissolving.
- Topic: mass of solute from temperature rise of solution (q = mcΔT → n → m).
  the temperature increases from 20.4 °C to 23.2 °C... Calculate the mass of
  CaCl2(s)." (ΔH = −81.3 kJ mol⁻¹, 68.0 g solution, M = 111.)
- Answer: q = 68.0 × 4.18 × 2.8 = 795.9 J; n = 0.7959/81.3 = 9.79×10⁻³ mol;
  m = 9.79×10⁻³ × 111 = 1.09 g.
- Verified: every step re-done independently + schedule working identical;
  A = one step, M = process one error, E = correct answer. HIGH.

## 91390-2025-Q2ci · 2025 Exam Q2(c)(i) · Achieved→Excellence

- Location: 91390-exm-2025.pdf, Question Two (c)(i), rocket fuel.
- Topic: ΔrH° via Hess's law from formation/vapourisation data.
  Calculate the standard enthalpy change." (Data: ΔfH°(CH3NHNH2) = +54.1,
  ΔfH°(CO2) = −394, ΔfH°(N2O4) = −20.0, ΔfH°(H2O(l)) = −286,
  ΔvapH°(H2O) = +40.7.)
- Answer: (−54.1×4) + (−394×4) + (+20.0×5) + (−286×12) + (+40.7×12) =
  −4636 ≈ −4640 kJ mol⁻¹.
- Verified: arithmetic re-done independently + schedule identical.
  Tier: A = manipulating one equation, E = correct answer. HIGH.

## 91390-2022-Q1ci · 2022 Exam Q1(c)(i) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence
