# Verified question index — AS91927 Unfamiliar Texts (English L1)

How to use: the student works from their OWN copy of the NZQA paper and
resource booklet (free on nzqa.govt.nz). Give the LOCATION (year + question
+ text title), never a reconstructed full text. Confirm identification with
at most 2 lines of stem. Grade against the schedule criteria ladder below.

Format per entry: ID | location | topic | criteria ladder | verification.

Format note: questions are whole-response and assessed holistically
(N1-E8), not banded per question. Verified tier ladder for every entry:
- Achieved: explain how ONE significant aspect creates meaning/effect.
- Merit ("convincing"): explain how TWO OR MORE aspects work together.
- Excellence ("perceptive"): explain how aspects are deliberately used to
  communicate the writer's purpose and wider contexts.

The unfamiliar texts themselves are printed in 91927-res-<year>.pdf;
per the location-only policy this index never carries text or quotes.
