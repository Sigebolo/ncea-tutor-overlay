## 91391-2025-Q1b · 2025 Exam Q1(b) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91391-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (b), reaction scheme.
- Topic: multi-step synthesis, propanal → N-propylpropanamide.
  N-propylpropanamide" (reagents limited to SOCl2, Cr2O7 2–/H+, NaBH4,
  conc NH3).
- COURSE-COHERENT ROUTE (teach from this split, not one line):
    1. Propanal →(NaBH4) propan-1-ol  (aldehyde → primary alcohol)
    2. Propan-1-ol →(SOCl2) 1-chloropropane  (alcohol → alkyl halide)
    3. 1-chloropropane + conc NH3 → propylamine  (alkyl halide → primary amine;
       course-known N-alkylation; product mixture is realistic, but a primary
       amine is obtainable in outline)
    4. Propanal →(Cr2O7 2–/H+, heat) propanoic acid  (aldehyde oxidation here;
       this is making the ACID side, not extending the chain and not the
       amide-forming step)
    5. Propanoic acid →(SOCl2) propanoyl chloride; propanoyl chloride +
       propylamine → N-propylpropanamide  (acid/derivative + amine = amide)
  In exam terms the task is a constrained multi-step synthesis: both halves
  (amine chain and acid/acyl side) must be built from the same starting
  material under the given reagent list, then joined into the amide.
- Verified: paper and stem confirmed (91391-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (b),
  reagents SOCl2, Cr2O7 2–/H+, NaBH4, conc NH3); route re-solved under the

## 91391-2022-Q1b · 2022 Exam Q1(b) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91391-exm-2022.pdf, Question One (b), three unlabelled bottles.
- Topic: identify propanoyl chloride / propan-1-ol / propanoic acid.
  colourless liquids using only... water, litmus paper, acidified potassium
  dichromate solution."
- Answer: water → propanoyl chloride reacts vigorously with fumes (HCl on

- Topic: naming and explaining the condensation reaction that forms a peptide
  bond.
  formation of the dipeptide from the two amino acids."
- Answer: condensation (amide/peptide-bond formation); the amine group of one

## 91391-2025-Q1aiii · 2025 Exam Q1(a)(iii) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91391-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (a)(iii), Tollens' test on D-glucose.
- Topic: aldehyde functional group, oxidation by Tollens' reagent, silver mirror.
  Explain this observation with reference to the functional group involved and the type of
  reaction occurring."
- Answer: D-glucose contains an aldehyde group. On heating, the aldehyde group is oxidised
  to a carboxylic acid group, while the silver ions in Tollens' reagent are reduced to silver
  atoms, which deposit as a silver mirror.
- Tier/marks: Achievement — identifies the presence of the aldehyde group and classifies
  the reaction as oxidation; Merit — explains the aldehyde group is oxidised to a carboxylic
  acid; Excellence — as Merit plus the silver ions being reduced to silver atoms.
- Verified: question verified in 91391-exm-2025.pdf Q1(a)(iii); model answer and all three
  tier bullets read directly from 91391-ass-2025.pdf Q (a)(iii), page 1. HIGH.
