## 91159-2022-Q2 · 2022 Exam Q2(a)–(b) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91159-exm-2022.pdf, Question Two (a) and (b), mutations and albinism.
- Topic: definition of mutation; insertion, deletion, substitution; silent mutations and
  degeneracy; frameshift effects on the final protein.
  mutations on final proteins" — describe an insertion, deletion and substitution mutation;
  name the mutation unlikely to change the protein and explain why; name the mutation that
  would change the protein the most and explain why; discuss how these mutations affect the
  length and expression of the DNA base sequence; discuss the degeneracy in the genetic
  code.
- Answer: (a) a permanent change in the DNA sequence / genetic code / gene.
  (b) Achievement — insertion = a base added to the sequence; deletion = a base removed;
  substitution = one nucleotide swapped; describes degeneracy of the code; identifies
  substitution as unlikely to change the protein and insertion/deletion as most likely to.
  Merit — substitution has least effect because it may code for the same amino acid (silent)
  / does not cause a frameshift / leaves length and start-stop codons unchanged; insertion
  has a major effect because of the frameshift; deletion has a major effect because of the
  frameshift. Excellence — insertion/deletion change every codon after the mutation point,
  altering the amino acid sequence and possibly the stop codon, so the polypeptide folds
  differently and the protein loses function; substitution changes at most one amino acid, so
  the shape change is slight; the degree of change in folding is what matters for function.
- Tier/marks: no per-part marks printed; tier columns for Q TWO (a), (b) as summarised
  (91159-ass-2022.pdf p3).
- Verified: question verified in 91159-exm-2022.pdf Q2(a)-(b); all tier bullets read directly
  from 91159-ass-2022.pdf Q TWO (a), (b). HIGH.
