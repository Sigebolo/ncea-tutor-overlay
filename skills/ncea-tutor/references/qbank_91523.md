## 91523-2025-Q3a · 2025 Exam Q3(a) · Achieved (SHOW)

- Location: 91523-exm-2025.pdf, Question Three (a), double-slit SHOW.
- Topic: laser wavelength from fringe spacing (nλ = dx/L).
  maxima is 4.20 × 10–2 m. Show that the laser has a wavelength of 515 nm."
  (d = 1.25×10⁻⁴ m, L = 3.40 m.)
- Answer: 3λ = 1.25×10⁻⁴ × 0.042/3.40 → λ = 5.15×10⁻⁷ m = 515 nm.
