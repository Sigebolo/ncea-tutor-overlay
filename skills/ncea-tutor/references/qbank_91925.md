# Verified question index — AS91925 Studied Text (English L1)

How to use: the student works from their OWN copy of the NZQA paper
(free on nzqa.govt.nz). Give the LOCATION (year + question), never
a reconstructed full text. Confirm identification with at most 2 lines of
stem. Grade against the schedule criteria ladder below.

Format per entry: ID | location | topic | criteria ladder | verification.

Format note: candidates answer ONE essay question from a choice of four,
discussing at least TWO specific aspects of their studied text(s).
Questions are whole-response and assessed on criteria (N1-E8), not banded
per question. Verified tier ladder for every entry:
- Achieved: describe aspects and how they create engagement/viewpoints.
- Merit ("convincing"): explain how the aspects work together, with details.
- Excellence ("perceptive"): discuss deliberate use + creator's purpose or
  wider contexts (human experience, society, the wider world).
