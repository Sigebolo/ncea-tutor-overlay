## 91526-2024-Q2b · 2024 Exam Q2(b) · Achieved/Merit

- Location: 91526-exm-2024.pdf, Question Two (b), coil entering a field.
- Topic: EMF from changing flux (N·ΔΦ/Δt).
  of flux density 1.80 Wb m–2 ... It takes 0.300 s for the coil to be placed
  in the magnetic field. Calculate the EMF induced in the coil."
- Answer: ΔΦ = 1.80 × 1.60×10⁻⁴ = 2.88×10⁻⁴ Wb; EMF = 25 × 2.88×10⁻⁴ / 0.300
  = 0.0240 V.
- Verified: arithmetic re-done + schedule: A = ONE of (ΔΦ etc.), Merit =
  V = 2.40×10⁻² V. HIGH.
