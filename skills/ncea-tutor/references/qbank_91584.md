## 91584-2025-Q1a · 2025 Exam Q1(a)(i) · Achieved

- Location: 91584-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (a)(i); resource in 91584-res-2025.pdf.
- Topic: margin of error calculation for survey.
  Show how this margin of error was calculated."
- Answer/criteria: MOE = 1/sqrt(n) where n = sample size. For n = 2000 (from resource),
  MOE = 1/sqrt(2000) ≈ 0.0224 ≈ 2.2%. Achievement: show correct formula and calculation.
  Merit: explain what MOE means in context. Excellence: discuss factors affecting MOE
  (sample size, confidence level).
- Tier/marks: N1/N2, A3/A4, M5/M6, E7/E8 (per 91584-ass-2025.pdf).
- Verified: question verified in 91584-exm-2025.pdf; resource data verified;

## 91584-2025-Q1b · 2025 Exam Q1(b) · Achieved/Merit

- Location: 91584-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (b); resource in 91584-res-2025.pdf.
- Topic: confidence interval for proportion of Americans polite to AI.
  Construct and interpret a confidence interval for the proportion of Americans who are
  polite when communicating with AI."
- Answer/criteria: Given p̂ = 1240/2000 = 0.62, n = 2000. 95% CI: p̂ ± MOE = 0.62 ± 0.022
  = (0.598, 0.642). Achievement: calculate p̂ correctly, state formula, construct interval.
  Merit: interpret interval in context ("we are 95% confident that the true proportion of
  Americans who are polite to AI is between 59.8% and 64.2%"). Excellence: discuss validity
  conditions, implications of "representative" assumption.
- Tier/marks: N1/N2, A3/A4, M5/M6, E7/E8 (per 91584-ass-2025.pdf).
- Verified: question verified in 91584-exm-2025.pdf; resource data verified (1240/2000);
  criteria verified in 91584-ass-2025.pdf. HIGH.

## 91584-2025-Q1c · 2025 Exam Q1(c) · Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91584-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (c); resource in 91584-res-2025.pdf.
- Topic: comparing NZ and US politeness towards AI using confidence intervals.
  624 New Zealanders are polite when communicating with AI. Can the following claim be made
  from the results of these surveys? New Zealanders are more polite when communicating with
  AI than Americans. Construct ONE confidence interval, and interpret this interval as part
  of your answer."
- Answer/criteria: NZ: p̂ = 624/1250 = 0.4992 ≈ 0.50. n = 1250. MOE = 1/sqrt(1250) ≈ 0.0283.
  95% CI: 0.50 ± 0.028 = (0.472, 0.528). US CI from Q1(b): (0.598, 0.642). The intervals
  do not overlap, so the claim is supported. Achievement: calculate NZ p̂ and CI correctly.
  Merit: interpret NZ interval, compare with US interval, make claim with justification.
  Excellence: discuss limitations (different sample sizes, different populations, assumption
  of independence), evaluate strength of evidence.
- Tier/marks: N1/N2, A3/A4, M5/M6, E7/E8 (per 91584-ass-2025.pdf).
- Verified: question verified in 91584-exm-2025.pdf; resource data verified (624/1250);
  criteria verified in 91584-ass-2025.pdf. HIGH.
