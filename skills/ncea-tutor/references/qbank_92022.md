## 92022-2025-Q1a · 2025 Exam Q1(a) · Q1 total 8 marks · Merit

- Location: 92022-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (a).
- Topic: relationship between chromosomes, genes, alleles and DNA (with examples).
- Answer: Chromosomes are long strands of DNA tightly wound; DNA is a double helix of sugar, phosphate and bases (A–T, G–C). A gene is a specific section of the DNA sequence coding for a trait; an allele is a different version of the same gene, giving variation in the phenotype. Example: chromosome 7 carries the CFTR gene; a base change in it gives the mutant allele (f) instead of the normal allele (F).
- Verified: all four definitions + the CFTR example cross-read from the 2025 schedule; no invented content. HIGH.

## 92022-2025-Q2b · 2025 Exam Q2(b) · Q2 total 8 marks · Merit/Excellence

- Location: 92022-exm-2025.pdf, Question Two (b).
- Topic: confirm individual 7's genotype using parents' and children's evidence (Punnett squares).
- Answer: 7 is Ff. Parents 1 and 2 must both be Ff — 7 is unaffected (so 7 inherited at least one F) and 1 and 2 have affected children 5 and 6 (so each parent must carry f). 7 and 8 must also both be Ff, since they are unaffected yet have affected children 11 and 12. A cross of Ff × Ff gives FF : Ff : ff = 1 : 2 : 1, consistent with 7 being Ff. Excellence needs all three lines of evidence (parents 1–2, sibs 5–6, offspring 11–12).
- Verified: cross-read from the 2025 schedule's full evidence paragraph; the 1:2:1 ratio independently derived from an Ff × Ff Punnett square. HIGH.

## 92022-2025-Q3b · 2025 Exam Q3(b) · Q3 total 8 marks · Excellence

- Location: 92022-exm-2025.pdf, Question Three (b).
- Topic: why the recessive f allele persists in the population without CF phenotype rising.
- Answer: f is disadvantageous only in ff (affected) individuals and neutral in heterozygotes, so carriers keep passing it on; heterozygotes may even have a survival advantage against diseases such as cholera/typhoid, and in small populations non-random mating raises the chance of two carriers mating. Because F completely masks f, the number of f alleles (carried mainly in Ff) can rise while the number of CF phenotypes stays low.
- Verified: all reasoning points cross-read from the 2025 schedule's Excellence column (incl. the express statement that F masking f allows more Ff without more ff phenotypes). HIGH.
