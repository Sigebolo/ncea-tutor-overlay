## 91170-2022-Q2a · 2022 Exam Q2(a) · Achieved

- Topic: wavelength of purple light from frequency (v = fλ).
  Calculate the wavelength of the purple light."

- Verified: arithmetic re-done + schedule Achievement column "• Correct."

## 91170-2022-Q1d · 2022 Exam Q1(d) · Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91170-exm-2022.pdf, Question One (d), pin + concave lens.
- Topic: virtual image from a concave lens; why it cannot form on a screen.
  lens... The lens has a focal length of 3 cm."
- Answer: 1/di = 1/f − 1/do = −1/3 − 1/7 → di = −2.1 cm; image is virtual,
  2.1 cm in front of the lens, so it can never appear on a screen.
- Verified: arithmetic re-done + schedule: A = two correct rays or substitution
  (di = 5.25 cm if f taken positive), M = any TWO of diagram/calculation/
  explanation, E = full answer. HIGH.

## 91170-2024-Q1b · 2024 Exam Q1(b) · Merit

- Location: 91170-exm-2024.pdf, Question One (b), waves running into a wall.
- Topic: wave speed from f and λ; speed change across the depth boundary.
  6 waves in 8.0 seconds... wavelength 4.0 cm reduces to 2.0 cm."
- Answer: f = 6/8 = 0.75 Hz; deep: v = 0.75×0.04 = 0.03 m s⁻¹; shallow:
  v2 = 0.03×(2.0/4.0) = 0.015 m s⁻¹.
- Verified: arithmetic re-done + schedule: A = correct frequency,
  Merit = correct answer. HIGH.

## 91170-2025-Q2c · 2025 Exam Q2(c) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91170-exm-2025.pdf, Question Two (c), red laser into glass.
- Topic: refractive index from wavelength (n = λ_air/λ_glass) and Snell's law.
  incident angle of 30°. The wavelength of the red light in the glass is 4.64 × 10–7 m.
  Calculate the angle of refraction as the red laser passes from air (n = 1.0) into the glass."
- Answer: n_glass = 650/464 = 1.4 (0.7138 for the ratio used). Snell: 0.7138 × sin 30° =
  sin i_glass → i_glass = 20.9° ≈ 21°.
- Tier/marks: Achievement — correct formula identified OR n_glass = 1.4 OR correct ratio;
  Merit — calculation with one error; Excellence — full calculation with the correct angle.
- Verified: question verified in 91170-exm-2025.pdf Q2(c); worked answer (n = 650/464,
  i = 20.9 ≈ 21°) and all three tier bullets read directly from 91170-ass-2025.pdf Q TWO (c). HIGH.
