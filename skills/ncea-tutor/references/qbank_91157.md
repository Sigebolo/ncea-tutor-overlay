## 91157-2022-Q1c · 2022 Exam Q1(c) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91157-exm-2022.pdf, Question One (c), linked and unlinked genes.
- Topic: independent assortment and crossing over — effect on linked/unlinked genes and on
  genetic variation.
  genes and unlinked genes AND how they both affect genetic variation in a population",
  covering: description of linked AND unlinked genes; the processes of independent
  assortment and crossing over including when they occur; why linked genes do not
  independently assort; a comparison/contrast of their effects on genetic variation.
- Answer: Achievement — linked genes are on the same chromosome (inherited together);
  unlinked genes are on different chromosomes or far apart on one chromosome; genetic
  variation = differences in alleles in a population/gene pool; describe independent
  assortment; state when IA/crossing over occurs (metaphase 1 / meiosis 1); describe
  crossing over. Merit — IA lines homologous pairs randomly at the equator in metaphase 1,
  so one chromosome of each pair enters each gamete, giving each gamete a different
  chromosome combination; how IA affects unlinked genes (they assort → recombinant
  gametes → more variation) and linked genes (same chromosome → cannot assort → fewer
  recombinants, offspring like parental generation); how crossing over affects linked genes
  far apart vs close together, and unlinked genes; how IA OR crossing over affects genetic
  variation. Excellence — thorough comparison/contrast of IA of linked vs unlinked genes and
  of crossing over of linked vs unlinked genes, and of linked vs unlinked genes overall:
  linked genes reduce variation, unlinked genes increase it; crossing over exchanges segments
  between homologous chromosomes (metaphase 1 / meiosis 1), reshuffling alleles and
  increasing variation, and can separate linked genes that lie far apart.
- Tier/marks: no per-part marks printed; Achievement / Merit / Excellence columns for
  Q ONE (c) as summarised above (91157-ass-2022.pdf pp2-3).
- Verified: question verified in 91157-exm-2022.pdf Q1(c); all three tier columns read
  directly from 91157-ass-2022.pdf Q ONE (c). HIGH.
