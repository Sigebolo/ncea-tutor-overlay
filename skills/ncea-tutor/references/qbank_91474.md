## 91474-2025-Q1 · 2025 Exam Q1 (Text A "Lippy") · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91474-exm-2025.pdf, Question One; text in 91474-res-2025.pdf p.2, Text A
  "Lippy".
- Topic: ways in which storytelling is explored in the passage.
  referring to at least TWO specific aspects of written texts."
- Answer/criteria: Achievement — respond critically to significant aspects of the text with
  supporting evidence; identify at least TWO specific aspects of written texts (e.g.,
  language features, structure, point of view); show awareness of how storytelling is
  explored. Merit — respond critically and convincingly; present reasoned discussion of how
  the specified aspects explore storytelling; show clear understanding. Excellence — respond
  critically and perceptively; develop insightful argument about how storytelling is explored
  through the aspects; show awareness of deliberate crafting for purpose.
- Tier/marks: N1/N2, A3/A4, M5/M6, E7/E8 (cut scores per 91474-ass-2025.pdf).
- Verified: question wording verified in 91474-exm-2025.pdf Q1; text reference verified;
  criteria verified in 91474-ass-2025.pdf. HIGH.
