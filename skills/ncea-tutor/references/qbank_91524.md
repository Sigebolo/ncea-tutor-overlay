## 91524-2022-Q1b · 2022 Exam Q1(b) · Achieved/Merit

- Location: 91524-exm-2022.pdf, Question One (b), roundabout slowing down.
- Topic: angular deceleration and frictional torque.
  rotational inertia of 57.6 kg m2."
- Answer: ωf = 2π/2.04 = 3.08 rad s⁻¹; α = (3.08 − 5.11)/30.0 = −0.0677

  Merit = complete correct answer. HIGH.

## 91524-2024-Q2a · 2024 Exam Q2(a) · Achieved (SHOW)

- Location: 91524-exm-2024.pdf, Question Two (a), golf ball on a flywheel.
- Topic: angular acceleration from linear acceleration (α = a/r).
  1.83 m s–2. Show that the angular acceleration of the flywheel is 16.6 rad s–2."
  (Flywheel diameter 0.220 m.)
- Answer: α = a/r = 1.83 / 0.110 = 16.6 rad s⁻².
