## 91156-2022-Q1a · 2022 Exam Q1(a) · Achieved

  photosynthesis. Describe the reactants required for photosynthesis AND how they
  enter the plant."

## 91156-2022-Q1b · 2022 Exam Q1(b) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91156-exm-2022.pdf, Question One (b), chloroplast structures.
- Topic: how chloroplast structure supports the light-dependent and light-independent
  reactions (two named structures, structure→function, structures working together).
  light-independent reactions affect the amounts of the products of photosynthesis:
  describe the function of TWO named structures from the chloroplast diagram; explain how
  the structures of the chloroplast are built to help them carry out their function; discuss
  how the structures of the chloroplast work together to carry out the process of
  photosynthesis."
- Answer: Achievement — function of one structure (thylakoid/grana carries out the light
  reaction / captures light energy), function of an additional part (stroma carries out the
  dark reaction / captures CO2), how one structure is built for its job (e.g. clear membranes
  let light through). Merit — one structure explained and linked to its function (chlorophyll
  in grana absorbs light for the light reaction; stroma holds the enzymes for the dark
  reaction); two structures/reactions linked (clear membranes and liquid stroma let light in
  and CO2 diffuse to the grana); reactants and products linked (water split in the light
  reaction supplies hydrogen/O2, CO2 fixed in the light-independent reaction makes glucose).
  Excellence — a fully linked discussion of structures + reactants + products (grana and
  chlorophyll → light reaction → ATP/H+ driving the dark reaction to make glucose/release
  oxygen), and a second fully linked discussion (semi-permeable membranes + liquid stroma
  → CO2 transport and fixation → glucose).
- Tier/marks: no per-part marks printed; tier columns for Q ONE (b) as above, ladder on
  p2 of 91156-ass-2022.pdf.
- Verified: question verified in 91156-exm-2022.pdf Q1(b); Achievement/Merit/Excellence
  evidence read directly from 91156-ass-2022.pdf Q ONE (b). HIGH.
