## 91171-2022-Q2bi · 2022 Exam Q2(b)(i) · Merit

- Location: 91171-exm-2022.pdf, Question Two (b)(i).

- Answer: 0 = 0.5 × vr + 2 × 0.5 → 0.5vr = –1.0 → vr = –2.0 m s–1, i.e.
