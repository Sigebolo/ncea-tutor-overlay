# Verified question index — AS91100 Analyse significant aspects of unfamiliar written text(s) (English L2)

How to use: the student works from their OWN copy of the NZQA paper and resource
booklet (free on nzqa.govt.nz). Give the LOCATION (year + question + text), never
a reconstructed full text. Confirm identification with at most 2 lines of stem.
Grade against the schedule-verified criteria.

This standard requires close reading of unfamiliar texts: candidates attempt ALL
questions. Entries below are the question prompts (the "location" is the question
number plus text reference), verified against the exam paper and schedule.

Format per entry: ID | location | topic | answer/criteria | tier/marks | verification.
