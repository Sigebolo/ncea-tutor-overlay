# Verified question index — AS91099 Analyse specified aspect(s) of studied visual or oral text(s) (English L2)

How to use: the student works from their OWN copy of the NZQA paper
(free on nzqa.govt.nz). Give the LOCATION (year + question), never
a reconstructed full text. Confirm identification with at most 2 lines of
stem. Grade against the schedule-verified criteria.

This standard is an essay standard for visual or oral texts: candidates choose
ONE question and write an essay on at least one studied visual or oral text.
Entries below are the question prompts (the "location" is the question number
plus the wording of the prompt), verified against the exam paper and schedule.

Format per entry: ID | location | topic | answer/criteria | tier/marks | verification.

## 91099-2025-Q1 · 2025 Exam Q1 · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91099-exm-2025.pdf, Question 1.
- Topic: how verbal aspects were used to enhance your experience.
- Answer/criteria: Achievement — structured essay using analysis terminology; addresses chosen
  question/aspect; states a relevant idea; shows awareness of the use of verbal aspects;
  includes details from the text(s). Merit — convinces by engaging with the question/aspect;
  presents reasoned discussion of one or more ideas; shows clear understanding; awareness
  that effects/meanings are deliberately crafted. Excellence — perceptive; develops
  insightful/original argument; shows awareness that effects/meanings are deliberately
  crafted for a particular purpose; makes judgments about how effects/meanings relate to
  the purpose; weaves a range of relevant details to support the main idea(s).
- Tier/marks: N1/N2, A3/A4, M5/M6, E7/E8 (cut scores 0-2 NA, 3-4 A, 5-6 M, 7-8 E
  per 91099-ass-2025.pdf).
