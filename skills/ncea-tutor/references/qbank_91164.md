## 91164-2022-Q3cii · 2022 Exam Q3(c)(ii) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91164-exm-2022.pdf, Question Three (c)(ii), LiBr vs KBr in water.
- Topic: matching an energy change by choosing a mass of a second salt.
  Calculate the mass of solid KBr (ΔrH = +19.9 kJ mol–1) that would need to
  be dissolved to have an energy change of equal magnitude. M(LiBr) = 86.8,
  M(KBr) = 119."
- Answer: n(LiBr) = 20.0/86.8 = 0.230 mol; energy = 0.230 × 48.8 = 11.2 kJ;
  n(KBr) = 11.2/19.9 = 0.563 mol; m(KBr) = 0.563 × 119 = 67 g.
- Verified: arithmetic re-done + schedule Three (c)(ii) prints "0.230 mol",
  "11.2 kJ", "0.563 mol" with Excellence bullet "Correct mass of KBr
  calculated". Give 67 g (2sf) and accept 67.0 g. HIGH.

## 91164-2023-Q2a · 2023 Exam Q2(a) · Achieved/Merit/Excellence
