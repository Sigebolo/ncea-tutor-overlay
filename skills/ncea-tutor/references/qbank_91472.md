# Verified question index — AS91472 Respond critically to specified aspect(s) of studied written text(s) (English L3)

How to use: the student works from their OWN copy of the NZQA paper
(free on nzqa.govt.nz). Give the LOCATION (year + question), never
a reconstructed full text. Confirm identification with at most 2 lines of
stem. Grade against the schedule-verified criteria.

This standard is an essay standard: candidates choose ONE statement and write
an essay on at least one studied written text, responding critically to the
statement. Entries below are the statements (the "location" is the statement
number plus the wording), verified against the exam paper and schedule.

Format per entry: ID | location | topic | answer/criteria | tier/marks | verification.

## 91472-2025-Q1 · 2025 Exam Q1 · Achieved/Merit/Excellence

- Location: 91472-exm-2025.pdf, Statement 1.
- Topic: characters who behave badly make us think deeply.
- Answer/criteria: Achievement — respond critically to the statement with reference to
  specified aspect(s); support response with evidence from studied text(s); show awareness
  of the statement's implications. Merit — respond critically and convincingly; present
  reasoned discussion; show clear understanding of how the text(s) engage with the statement;
  support with relevant evidence. Excellence — respond critically and perceptively; develop
  insightful/original argument; show awareness of multiple perspectives; make judgments
  about how the text(s) support or challenge the statement; weave a range of relevant
  evidence to support the argument.
- Tier/marks: N1/N2, A3/A4, M5/M6, E7/E8 (cut scores per 91472-ass-2025.pdf).
