## 91946-2025-Q2c · 2025 Exam Q2(c) · Q2 total 8 marks · Excellence

- Location: 91946-exm-2025.pdf, Question Two (c).
- Topic: GST/discount/shipping — compare three companies' true cost and recommend (resource 2B prices $68 / $90 / $70).
- Answer: one pair → Footy Fields, $80.34; two pairs → Kick Off, $124.75.
  - Football Direct: 68 × 1.15 = 78.20; + 6.66 = $84.86. Two pairs: 156.40.
  - Footy Fields: 90 × 0.95 = 85.50; ÷1.15 = 74.35; + 5.99 = $80.34. Two pairs: 180 × 0.90 = 162; ÷1.15 = 140.87; + 5.99 = $146.86.
  - Kick Off: 70 × 1.15 = 80.50; + 4 = $84.50 (or $80.50 collecting). Two pairs: (70 + 35) × 1.15 = 120.75; + 4 = $124.75.
- Verified: independently recomputed all three companies for both one and two pairs; matches the 2025 schedule figure-for-figure ("best one pair Footy Fields $80.34; best two pairs Kick Off $124.75"). HIGH.
