## 92023-2025-Q1b · 2025 Exam Q1(b) · Q1 total 8 marks · Merit

- Location: 92023-exm-2025.pdf, Question One (b).
- Topic: LDPE structure → low density → suitability for a kayak.
- Answer: Polyethylene is long, branched chains of covalently bonded carbon atoms with weak intermolecular forces between the chains. The branching and the weak forces mean the chains do not pack tightly → low density → density less than water → floats. (Also insoluble: strong covalent bonds within the chain are not broken by water.)
- Verified: key marking points cross-read from the 2025 schedule (branched chains; weak intermolecular forces; not tightly packed; density < water; insoluble) — the whole-part answer is a structure→property→use chain. HIGH.

## 92023-2025-Q2bii · 2025 Exam Q2(b)(ii) · Q2 total 8 marks · Merit/Excellence

- Location: 92023-exm-2025.pdf, Question Two (b)(ii).
- Topic: why pure aluminium is more malleable than its alloy.
- Answer: Pure aluminium has cations all the same size, so layers slide over each other easily without breaking the metallic bond (attraction between cations and delocalised electrons). An alloy has at least two different-sized atoms (e.g. larger Mg), which disrupts the regular lattice — it is harder to push the irregular arrangement over itself, so the alloy is less malleable.
- Verified: marking points cross-read from the 2025 schedule ("same size … slide over … without breaking the attraction"; "different sizes … harder to push the irregular arrangement over each other"). HIGH.

## 92023-2025-Q3b · 2025 Exam Q3(b) · Q3 total 8 marks · Achieved/Merit

- Location: 92023-exm-2025.pdf, Question Three (b).
- Topic: ionic lattice — why salt (NaCl) is brittle.
- Answer: NaCl is a 3D lattice of alternating Na⁺ and Cl⁻ held by strong ionic bonds. When stress is applied the ion layers shift slightly, bringing like-charged ions next to each other; like charges repel and the lattice shatters.
- Verified: marking points cross-read from the 2025 schedule (3D lattice of oppositely charged ions; strong ionic bonds; layers shift; like-charges repel; lattice shatters). HIGH.

## 92023-2024-Q1b · 2024 Exam Q1(b) · Q1 total 8 marks · Merit

- Location: 92023-exm-2024.pdf, Question One (b).
- Topic: graphite structure and bonding → softness and electrical conduction.
- Answer: Graphite is a 2D covalent network; each carbon bonds to three others in layers, with the fourth electron delocalised between the layers. The layers are held by weak attractive forces, so little energy transfers layers to the paper (soft, Moh 1–2). The delocalised electrons are free to move, so the pencil line conducts.
- Verified: marking points cross-read from the 2024 schedule (2D covalent network; 3 bonds per C; 4th electron delocalised; weak forces between layers; layers slide/transfer; free-moving electrons enable current). HIGH.
