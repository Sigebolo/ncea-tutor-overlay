# 7-Week Revision Plan — Level 2 Chemistry (externals)

Exam-ready plan targeting **Excellence**. Materials referenced live in
`nzqa_materials/Chemistry_L2/AS*/`.

**Standards:** AS 91164 Bonding, Structure, Properties & Energy · AS 91165
Organic Compounds · AS 91166 Chemical Reactivity (all external, 4/3/4 credits).

## Weekly structure

| Week | Focus | DeepTutor activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 91164: Lewis structures, shapes, polarity; 2021–22 papers | KB `NZQA_C2_AS91164_Bonding`; quiz on structure/shape explanations |
| 2 | 91164: enthalpy & bond energy calculations + E-level justifications; 2023–25 papers | Timed drills; grade vs schedule; "why does shape follow from VSEPR" chains |

| 6 | 91166 E-level: equilibrium justifications with collision theory; full timed papers | Timed 2021–25 papers; error-log review |
| 7 | Integration across all three: 2 timed papers/week, weak-spot repair | Question Bank review; descriptor self-checklist |

## The Excellence habit (from the assessment reports)

- Structure→property→use reasoning chains (e.g. polarity explains solubility
  explains...); markers reward the *link*, not the isolated fact.
- Equilibrium answers must reference **both** forward/reverse rates or
  relative species amounts, plus collision/energy reasoning for E.
- Organic: state reagent AND condition AND observation; E needs the
  structure-based justification.

## Weekly routine

- 3 sessions × 45 min + 1 timed block/week (from week 2, since Chem papers
  are shorter).
- Every session: 10 min error-log flash review, 25 min content/drills,
  10 min "explain-why" practice against schedule wording.
