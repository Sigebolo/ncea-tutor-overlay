## The Excellence habit (from assessment reports)

- Calculus E answers require **setting up** models from context, correct
  technique selection under pressure, and interpreted conclusions ("hence the
  maximum volume is...").
- Show the *method* marks: correct notation, limits handled properly,
  constants of integration.
- Statistics E needs the full cycle with **justified links to population and
  context**, uncertainty discussion, and correct formal conditions.

## Weekly routine

- 4 sessions × 45 min (L3 math is the biggest credit load) + 1 timed paper
  from week 3.
- Every session: error-log flash review, technique drills, one "explain why
  this method applies" item.
