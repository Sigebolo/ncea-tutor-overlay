# 7-Week Revision Plan — Level 2 Physics (externals)

Exam-ready plan targeting **Excellence**. Each external is one exam paper;
the plan interleaves content review, past-paper drilling, and E-level
explanation training. Materials referenced live in
`nzqa_materials/Physics_L2/AS*/`.

**Standards:** AS 91170 Waves · AS 91171 Mechanics · AS 91173 Electricity &
Electromagnetism (all external, 4–6 credits each). AS 91172 Atomic & Nuclear is
internal — skill-practice support only, no exam drill.

## The Excellence habit (what markers look for)

From the assessment reports (2021–2025, in each KB):

- E answers **explain mechanisms**, not just calculate: energy conservation
  reasoning, Newton's laws applied to systems, flux-change → induced EMF chains.
- Final answers with **units, sig figs, and a statement linking back to the
  physical situation**.
- For "discuss/justify" items: identify the principle → apply it → state the
  consequence, in that order.

## Weekly routine (suggested)

- 3 sessions × 45 min + 1 timed paper block/week (from week 4).
- Every session: 10 min flash-review of previous error log (DeepTutor memory),
  25 min new content/drills, 10 min E-explanation practice (say-it-out-loud
  mechanism explanation, tutor checks against schedule wording).
